# PARKLAB 3D — Anjila's 1-Minute Demo Script (60 Seconds)
## Parking Challenges, Deterministic Assessment, AI Crash RAG Coach & Gameplay UI

> **Target Duration:** 60 seconds (~140 words)

---

### Beat 1: The 5 UK Parking Challenges & 3D Scene (`0:00 – 0:20`)

**[ON SCREEN]:**
- Open `/challenges` showing the 5 UK manoeuvre cards, then click **Parallel Parking** to launch straight into `/simulation` with its custom soundtrack.

**[SAY]:**
> *"Hi, I'm Anjila, and I built the **Parking Challenges, Assessment Engine, AI Crash Coach, and Gameplay UI**. Here in the Challenge Catalogue are all five UK driving test manoeuvres—clicking any card launches straight into the 3D scene with its own custom soundtrack, bay markings, kerbs, cones, and parked cars."*

---

### Beat 2: Intentional Crash + RAG & Voice AI Coach (`0:20 – 0:40`)

**[ON SCREEN]:**
- Drive with `W/A/S/D` and **clip the front parked car**.
- Let the music duck as the **Voice Coach** speaks, and point to the compact **`CRASH`** card + **Craft Lab & AI Coach** (webcam cue & *Ask Coach*).

**[SAY]:**
> *"As I drive, my HUD tracks live MPH, alignment, and 4-corner bay containment. When I clip this parked car, the music ducks so our **Voice Coach** can speak the fix aloud. Using a 60-frame rolling buffer and a **TF-IDF RAG pipeline** backed by `localStorage` memory, it identifies my pre-impact speed, impact corner, cause, and fix—and also supports an opt-in **Webcam Observer** and voice Q&A."*

---

### Beat 3: Retry, Deterministic 4-Corner Score, 3D Replay & Mobile (`0:40 – 1:00`)

**[ON SCREEN]:**
- Click **Retry**, park inside the green bay, and press **`E` (Park & Evaluate)**.
- Show the **`ResultsPanel`** (`PASS`, score, stars, Attempt #2 vs #1 delta), then click **Watch Replay**.

**[SAY]:**
> *"When I click **Retry** and park cleanly inside the bay, my deterministic **`evaluateParking`** engine checks all four rotated corners, alignment angle, and kerb distance—comparing Attempt #2 against Attempt #1. From the results panel, I can launch a full **3D Action Replay**, and on mobile, the HUD minimizes with a **Left Brake and Right 360° Thumbstick**."*
